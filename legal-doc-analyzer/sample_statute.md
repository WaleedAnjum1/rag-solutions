# Sample Statute Document

## Title: Data Protection Act 2025

**Jurisdiction:** United Kingdom

**Section 1 – Definitions**
- "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, and disclosure.

**Section 2 – Rights of Data Subjects**
1. The right to access their personal data.
2. The right to request correction of inaccurate data.
3. The right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") under certain conditions.

**Section 3 – Obligations of Data Controllers**
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational measures.
- Conduct Data Protection Impact Assessments for high‑risk processing.
- Notify the Information Commissioners Office (ICO) of breaches.

**Section 4 – Enforcement**
- Failure to comply may result in fines up to £17.5 million or 4% of global annual turnover.

**Section 5 – Data Protection Officers**
- Required for public authorities and organisations processing large‑scale sensitive data.

**Section 6 – International Data Transfers**
- Data transfers outside the UK require appropriate safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

**Section 7 – Exemptions**
- Certain processing activities are exempt, including national security, defence, and law enforcement purposes.
