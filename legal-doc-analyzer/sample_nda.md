# Sample NDA

## Parties
- **Disclosing Party**: Acme Corp.
- **Receiving Party**: Beta LLC

## Purpose
The Receiving Party will receive confidential information for evaluating a potential partnership.

## Definition of Confidential Information
Any non‑public information disclosed orally or in writing, marked as confidential.

## Obligations
- The Receiving Party shall protect the confidential information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information.
- Use the information solely for the Purpose.

## Term
This Agreement remains effective for **2 years** from the Effective Date.

## Exclusions
Information that is publicly known, rightfully obtained from a third party, or independently developed.

## Return of Materials
Upon termination, the Receiving Party must return or destroy all confidential materials.

## Governing Law
State of California.

## Signatures

____________________   ____________________
Disclosing Party       Receiving Party
