# Sample Firm Policy

## Purpose
This policy outlines the firm's stance on **ethical conduct**, data privacy, and client confidentiality.

## Key Sections
- **Confidentiality:** Employees must not disclose client information without consent.
- **Anti‑Discrimination:** Zero tolerance for discrimination based on race, gender, religion, or sexual orientation.
- **Compliance:** Adherence to applicable statutes and regulations in each jurisdiction.

*This document serves as a representative example for the `firm_policy` legal document type.*
