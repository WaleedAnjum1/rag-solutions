# Regulation Example: Data Protection Act

## Section 1 – Purpose
This regulation establishes standards for the protection of personal data in electronic communications.

## Section 2 – Definitions
- **Personal Data**: Any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
- **Processor**: An entity that processes personal data on behalf of a controller.

## Section 3 – Obligations
1. Data controllers must obtain consent before processing personal data.
2. Processors must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure security.

## Section 4 – Enforcement
Violations are subject to fines up to 4% of annual global turnover or €20 million, whichever is greater.
