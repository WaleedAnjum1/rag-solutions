# Sample Employment Agreement

**Title:** Employment Agreement between Acme Corp and Jane Doe

## Parties
- **Employer:** Acme Corp, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of California.
- **Employee:** Jane Doe, residing at 123 Maple Street, San Francisco, CA.

## Effective Date
January 1, 2024

## Terms and Conditions
1. **Position**: Senior Software Engineer.
2. **Compensation**: $120,000 annual salary, paid bi‑weekly.
3. **Benefits**: Health insurance, 401(k) matching, 20 days paid vacation.
4. **Confidentiality**: Employee shall not disclose proprietary information.
5. **Termination**: Either party may terminate with 30‑day written notice.

## Sample Queries
- *What is the notice period for termination?*
- *What benefits are provided?*
- *When does the agreement become effective?*
