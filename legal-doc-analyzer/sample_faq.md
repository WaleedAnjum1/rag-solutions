# Acme SaaS Billing and Refund FAQ

## What is your refund policy?
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days from the date of approval. We offer a full refund if you cancel your subscription within the first 14 days of your initial purchase.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time by navigating to Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription in your dashboard. Your access will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle.

## Do you offer a free tier?
Yes, the Basic tier is completely free and allows up to 3 users and 100 API calls per month.

## Why was my credit card declined?
Common reasons for a declined card include expired payment methods, insufficient funds, or bank holds. Please contact your bank first. If the issue persists, reach out to our support team with error code ERR-CC-402.

## Can I pay annually instead of monthly?
Yes! Annual billing comes with a 20% discount compared to the monthly plan. You can switch your billing frequency in the Billing section of your dashboard.
