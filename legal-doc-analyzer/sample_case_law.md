# Sample Case Law

**Case:** *Smith v. Jones* (2022)

- **Jurisdiction:** Supreme Court of Exampleland
- **Key Holding:** The court affirmed that hate speech directed at a protected class constitutes a criminal act under the Hate Crimes Act.
- **Citation:** 123 Ex. 456 (2022)
- **Summary:** This case illustrates the application of hate crime statutes to online harassment.
