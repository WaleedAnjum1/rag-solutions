# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary information, technical data, trade secrets, software code, customer records, and commercial strategies disclosed by either party.

## 2. Term & Termination
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of two (2) years from the Effective Date. The confidentiality obligations regarding trade secrets shall survive indefinitely until the information becomes public without fault of the Receiving Party.

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

Neither party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, punitive, or consequential damages arising out of this Agreement.
