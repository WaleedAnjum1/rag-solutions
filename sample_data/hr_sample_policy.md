# Global Corporate Travel & Expense Policy

## 1. Overview and Purpose
This document outlines the corporate reimbursement rules and procedures for all employees traveling on official business. 

## 2. Daily Per Diem Allowances
- **Meals and Incidentals**: Employees are entitled to a daily per diem allowance of $75 for domestic business trips. International per diems vary by destination city (e.g., $110 for London, Tokyo, and Zurich).
- **Lodging**: Standard business hotel stays must not exceed $220/night before taxes unless prior written approval is granted by the department vice president.

## 3. Ground Transportation
Employees are encouraged to utilize public transit or rideshare services (Uber, Lyft) for standard business transit. Personal vehicle mileage for company travel will be reimbursed at the standard government rate ($0.67 per mile).

## 4. Expense Submission Timeline
All receipts and expense reports must be submitted within 14 calendar days following the conclusion of the trip via the internal HR portal. Reports submitted beyond 30 days will require executive CFO sign-off.
