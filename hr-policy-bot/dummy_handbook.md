# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## 1. Welcome to Acme Corp
Welcome to our team! We are thrilled to have you on board.

## 2. Working Hours
Standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. We support flexible schedules with core hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM.

## 3. Remote Work Policy
Employees can work remotely up to 3 days a week. Please coordinate with your manager for the specific remote days.

## 4. Paid Time Off (PTO)
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid time off per year, accrued monthly. Unused PTO up to 5 days can be rolled over to the next year.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement
All work-related expenses must be submitted via the HR portal within 30 days of the receipt. Approval typically takes 3-5 business days.

## 6. IT and Security
Use of personal devices for work is prohibited unless explicitly approved by the IT department. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
